RECOVERO 24/7 VEHICLE RECOVERY

Privacy Policy

Recovero 24/7 · Updated 18 October 2026

Recovero 24/7 respects your privacy. This policy explains how personal information is used when you visit our website, request vehicle recovery or transport, communicate with us, or use the Recovero Collector app as an approved recovery operator.

For data protection questions or requests, contact support@recovero247.co.uk or call 07366 302341.

1. Information we collect

Customers seeking vehicle recovery or transport

  • Contact details, such as your name and telephone number.
  • Vehicle details, including registration, make, model, condition and reported faults.
  • Pickup and destination addresses, job access instructions, and precise location when you choose to share it.
  • Booking details and references for deposits paid through secure third-party payment links.
  • Relevant communications and service updates.

Recovery partners and Recovero Collector app users

  • Name, business or trading name, work email address, telephone number, depot address and service coverage.
  • Approved account identifiers and information used to authenticate and manage app access.
  • Vehicle recovery capabilities, availability settings and trade rates.
  • Job offers, acceptance or decline decisions, alternative quotes, operator notes and job progress updates.
  • Completed-job records, payouts and earnings shown in the app.
  • A device-specific push notification token used to send incoming work alerts.

The Recovero Collector app does not require continuous GPS tracking of the operator's device. Customer addresses shown in assigned jobs are used for recovery and transport operations, not continuous location monitoring.

2. How we use personal information

  • To arrange and coordinate vehicle recovery and transport services.
  • To send necessary customer and vehicle information to the recovery operator handling a job.
  • To set up and protect approved collector accounts, verify logins and control access.
  • To offer recovery jobs, manage dispatch, communicate job updates and deliver push notifications.
  • To calculate and display agreed job rates, completion history and earnings.
  • To handle enquiries, complaints, refunds, security incidents and legal or accounting obligations.

We process information as appropriate to provide contracted services, meet legal obligations, pursue legitimate operational and security interests, or with your consent where required (for example, when you voluntarily share your location).

3. Who receives information

Where needed to perform recovery services, we share relevant customer contact, vehicle and route information with the recovery operator assigned to the job. Approved Recovero staff may access operational records necessary to manage and support jobs.

We use service providers to operate our systems, including Supabase for account authentication and backend storage, website and email hosting providers, and Apple Push Notification service for iOS alerts. These providers process information as needed to supply their services. We do not sell personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

Information may be processed or stored outside the UK by technology providers. Where required, we apply appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

4. Payment security

Booking deposits are collected using secure third-party payment links. Recovero does not store customers' full payment card numbers in its app or website systems. We may retain transaction references and amounts needed for booking administration, accounting or disputes.

5. Data security and retention

We use access controls, account authentication and other technical and organisational measures to restrict who can view customer and collector records. We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for operations, support, disputes and applicable legal or accounting obligations. When data is no longer needed, we delete it or anonymise it as appropriate.

6. Your UK data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can request access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of, or portability of personal data, or object to certain processing. You can also withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis. Some records must be retained where law or legitimate legal obligations require it.

To make a request, email support@recovero247.co.uk. If you are unhappy with our handling of your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

7. Cookies and website usage

Our website may use essential cookies or similar technologies required for secure website functions. Browser settings can be used to manage cookies. Any additional website analytics or advertising technologies should be described in our cookie information where applicable.

8. Privacy questions and changes

For questions about this policy or your collector account, contact support@recovero247.co.uk. We may update this document to reflect changes to our services, technology and legal requirements; the revision date at the top shows when it was last updated.
